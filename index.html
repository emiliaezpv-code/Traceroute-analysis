Traceroute analysis

Every line on this globe is a traceroute: a test that lists each router, or hop, a packet passes through on its way to a website. I ran them from home and plotted where those hops sit. The color of a line shows the destination, and brighter lines start at home while fainter ones start at school. Drag to spin the globe, scroll to zoom, hover a point to see its hop, and click a line to read what stands out about that path.

Click a line on the globe, or pick a trace above, to read what stands out about that path.

Points are city-level estimates placed from clues in hostnames, such as PHLAPA (Philadelphia) or lga (LaGuardia airport). Hops with no location clue, including timeouts, aren't drawn; you'll find them listed under the last point that was.
